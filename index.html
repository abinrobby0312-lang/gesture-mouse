What it does

Three ways to drive your computer

👆

Trackpad

Laptop-style: drag to move, tap to click, two fingers to scroll, two-finger tap to right-click, tap-and-hold to drag.

✋

Air gestures

Point the camera at your hand and don't touch anything: sweep, aim, pinch to grab, dip a finger to click. Tracking runs entirely on the phone.

⌨️

Keyboard

Type on the computer with your phone's own keyboard — swipe typing and voice typing included.

🎛️

Made to fit you

Speed, scrolling and themes are adjustable, and a sensitivity tracker watches how you move and suggests a better speed.

Set up in two minutes

Install and connect

  1. Download on your phone

    Use the button at the top of this page on your Android phone.

  2. Allow the install

    Android asks whether your browser may install apps — tap Settings, allow it, and go back. You'll see this for any app that isn't from the Play Store.

  3. Open it and keep it on screen

    Grant Bluetooth (and Camera if you want air gestures).

  4. Pair from your computer

    On the computer: Bluetooth settings → Add device → pick your phone → confirm the code. The dot turns green when it's connected. Next time, just open the app.

Questions

FAQ

Why does Android warn me before installing?
Android shows that for every app installed outside the Play Store. The app is signed with the same key every release, and each download lists its SHA-256 fingerprint so you can check the file is the real one.
Does it collect or upload anything?
No. The app has no internet permission at all — it physically can't send data anywhere. Hand tracking happens on the phone and the camera feed is never stored. The only data we hold is what you choose to send through the forms on this site. Privacy policy.
Which computers does it work with?
Anything that accepts a Bluetooth mouse. It's tested on Windows. macOS, Linux, Chromebooks, iPads and Android TVs should work the same way — they're being tested with the beta group now.
It paired but won't connect, or the keyboard doesn't type
Remove the pairing on both the computer and the phone, open the app, keep it on screen, and pair again from the computer. Computers remember what a device can do from the moment it paired — pairing while the app was closed, or with an older version, leaves them thinking it isn't a mouse or keyboard.
Does it work with the app in the background?
No — keep it on screen while you use it. Android only lets an app act as a Bluetooth mouse while it's open. Reopen it and it reconnects by itself.
Some symbols come out wrong when I type
The keyboard sends keys in the US layout. If your computer is set to another layout (French, German…), a few symbols land on different keys. Letters and numbers are fine. Characters without a US key, like é or emoji, are skipped.
How do I update?
Download the new version from this page and install it over the old one — your settings stay. Join the list below and we'll email you when there's a new version.
Is there an iPhone version?
No — iPhones don't allow apps to act as a Bluetooth mouse or keyboard.
