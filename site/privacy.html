Privacy policy

What we collect, and why

Last updated 18 September 2026.

Short version: the app collects nothing and can't — it has no internet access. This website only stores what you choose to type into its forms, uses it only for the purpose stated, and deletes it when you ask.

The app

  • The Android app does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • The camera feed for air gestures is processed on the phone, frame by frame, and never stored or transmitted.
  • The app keeps one small file on your phone with gesture timings, used to tune how gestures are recognised. It contains no images and no personal information, stays on the phone, and is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Settings (speed, theme and so on) are stored on the phone.
  • If you tap Email us or Report compatibility in the app, it opens your own email app or this website with your phone model, Android version and app version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

This website

  • Update list — your email address and the time you signed up, so we can tell you about new versions. Used for nothing else, never sold or shared. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe.
  • Compatibility reports — the phone and computer details and the notes you enter. No name or email is asked for. Reviewed reports may appear, without any personal details, in the public list of phones.
  • Messages — your message, and your email address only if you give one so we can reply.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and no cookies. The pages load nothing from other sites; only the app download itself comes from GitHub.

Where it's kept

Form entries are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. The website can add entries but cannot read them back; only the maintainer can. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, and downloads are served from GitHub Releases.

How long

Update-list emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask for removal. Messages and reports are kept while they're useful for support and compatibility, and deleted on request.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything you've sent us, and withdraw consent to emails at any time. Use the contact form and say what you'd like — include the email address you signed up with if it's about the update list. We'll act on it within 30 days.

Where the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) or the GDPR applies to you, these rights are yours under that law as well.

Children

The update list isn't intended for children under 18. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before signing up.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. We'll email the update list about any change that affects how your email address is used.
